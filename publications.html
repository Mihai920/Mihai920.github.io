  • An extension operator of Roper-Suffridge and Graham-Kohr type
    Complex Variables and Elliptic Equations, 2025.
    Abstract

    In this paper, we generalize the Roper-Suffridge-Graham-Kohr extension operator, \(f\mapsto \Psi_{n,\alpha,\beta}(f)\), where \(f\) is a normalized locally univalent function on the unit disc of the complex plane and

    \[ \Psi_{n,\alpha,\beta}(f)(z)= \left( f(z_1), \left[\frac{f(z_1)}{z_1}\right]^{\alpha} \left[f'(z_1)\right]^{\beta} \tilde z \right), \]

    where \(z=(z_1,\tilde z)\) belongs to the unit ball of \(\mathbb C^n\). In the second section, we investigate the preservation of starlikeness and convexity under the new operator. The newly introduced operator provides a new method for constructing starlike mappings on the unit ball of \(\mathbb C^n\). We also improve previous preservation results for \(\Psi_{n,\alpha,\beta}\), propose an open problem, and present an introductory study of this problem.
